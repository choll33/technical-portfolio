## New scholarships added

1. **Social Change Fund United (SCFU) Scholarship for HBCU Business Students** (`scfu-hbcu-business`, category `howard-hbcu`). Founded by Chris Paul, Dwyane Wade, and Carmelo Anthony; awards $5,000 each to 8 HBCU students in business-related majors, with an essay on economic empowerment and social equity. No class-year restriction found — direct match for Mason as a Howard Finance major. Deadline has moved year to year (Nov 9, 2024; June 2, 2025; June 15, 2026) — set `deadline` to June 15, 2027 as a best estimate given the two most recent cycles both landed in June, flagged as an estimate in the note.
2. **SIA Financial Planning Scholarship** (`sia-financial-planning`, category `finance-major`). Funded by Southwest Investment Advisors via Bold.org; $1,000 for undergraduate sophomores/juniors/seniors in a finance-related field, no race/ethnicity or geography restriction. Directly matches Mason's class year and major. 2026 cycle deadline was July 5, 2026 (closed); estimated July 5, 2027 for the next cycle as a recurring annual Bold.org listing.
