## New scholarships added (2)

1. **Ritchie-Jennings Memorial Scholarship Program (ACFE Foundation)** (`ritchie-jennings-acfe`, `finance-major`). $2,000–$10,000 from the Association of Certified Fraud Examiners Foundation. Finance is an explicitly named eligible major (alongside accounting, business administration, and criminal justice), and the program explicitly allows students "at least in the second half of sophomore year" — which is exactly where Mason will be by the application window. The 2026-2027 cycle deadline was Feb 2, 2026 (closed); set to Feb 1, 2027 as an estimate for the 2027-2028 cycle based on that recurring pattern. This is a strong new match — no race/ethnicity restriction, explicit finance-major eligibility, explicit sophomore eligibility, and a meaningful award amount.
2. **Central New Jersey Scholarship (Mensa Education and Research Foundation)** (`central-nj-mensa-scholarship`, `nj-resident`). $600 for NJ residents demonstrating leadership, any major. Verified via the Mensa Foundation's own scholarship FAQ that **Mensa membership is not required** to apply despite the sponsor name — a natural concern that would have been worth excluding over if true. Small award, but a direct NJ-residency match with no major restriction. The 2025-2026 cycle ran Sept 15, 2025 – Jan 15, 2026 (closed); Jan 15, 2027 set as the next-cycle estimate.
