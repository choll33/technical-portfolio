# Scholarship Research for Mason — Update, October 29, 2026

Follow-up to the October 28, 2026 research log. Mason remains a rising sophomore Finance major at Howard University (School of Business), GPA 3.7, NJ resident/hometown. Tracker held 63 entries entering this round.

This round focused on a sweep of major investment banks, asset managers, and private-equity-adjacent diversity programs not yet checked by name in prior logs (Blackstone, Barclays, Evercore, Lazard, Citadel, Jane Street, UBS, Deutsche Bank), plus a check on the UBS/PaineWebber UNCF-administered scholarship (to resolve whether it suffers the same UNCF-member-school restriction as the already-ruled-out UNCF General Scholarship) and New Jersey bank-foundation scholarships (New Jersey Bankers Education Foundation).

## Two new entries added

- **`altfinance-fellowship`** (category: `howard-hbcu`): AltFinance Fellowship, run by Management Leadership for Tomorrow (MLT) with Howard University as one of four **founding-partner HBCUs** (alongside Morehouse, Spelman, Clark Atlanta), building a pipeline of Black talent into alternative investments (PE, credit, real assets). Need-based funding each year a fellow stays in the program — historically up to $10,000 for sophomores — explicitly structured around the sophomore and junior years, a precise match for Mason's class year and Howard enrollment. The 2026-2027 cycle's deadline (April 15, 2026) has already passed; deadline set to an estimated April 15, 2027 based on that single confirmed cycle, flagged clearly as a one-cycle estimate rather than a confirmed multi-year pattern. Distinct from the already-tracked `mlt-career-prep` entry (MLT's broader Career Prep program, not Howard/HBCU-restricted and not alternative-investments-specific).
- **`evercore-diversity-scholarship`** (category: `black-finance`): Evercore Rising Junior Diversity Scholarship — $15,000 plus a guaranteed Summer Analyst investment-banking internship offer, for current sophomores from backgrounds underrepresented in finance (Black, Hispanic/Latinx, Native American, female, veteran, and/or LGBTQ+). Confirmed as a recurring program directly on Evercore's own careers page (evercore.com), though the only dated cycle found (2023: opened March 1, closed March 16) is now stale — deadline left `null` with a note to recheck evercore.com/careers starting ~February 2027. Like the already-tracked Morgan Stanley Fisher and JPMorgan Launching Leaders scholarships, this is gated behind completing Evercore's interview process and securing an internship offer, not a standalone cash-award application.

No existing entries' deadlines changed this round — the one deadline independently re-verified (TMCF | Enterprise Access HBCU Scholarship, already tracked at `2027-01-08`) was reconfirmed as correct directly against TMCF's own page and a third-party aggregator, with no change needed. Milken Institute HBCU Fellows Program's `null` deadline was also reconfirmed as still correct — no 2026-2027 deadline has been published anywhere, consistent with every prior session's finding.

## New leads investigated and ruled out (6 this round)

- **UBS/PaineWebber Scholarship (via UNCF)**: A real, sophomore/junior-eligible, business-field, need-based award — but confirmed restricted to the 37 UNCF **member** institutions specifically (not just HBCU status generally, unlike the already-tracked P&G HBCYOU/General, AlixPartners, Crum & Forster, UNCF Element Fleet, and UNCF MetLife Legacy awards). Howard is not a UNCF member school. **Ruled out — same reason as the already-excluded UNCF General Scholarship.**
- **Blackstone**: No Blackstone-sponsored scholarship (cash award) for HBCU or Black undergraduates was found. The one confirmed Blackstone HBCU initiative, Blackstone LaunchPad, is program/resource access (entrepreneurship coaching tools) at four HBCUs, not a scholarship. **Ruled out — no qualifying cash scholarship found.**
- **Barclays**: The one Barclays-funded HBCU program found (a $300,000 gift to the HBCU Week Foundation) was restricted to incoming high school seniors and its listing had already closed in 2024. Barclays' Empowering Leaders Fellowship targets underrepresented students broadly but isn't HBCU-specific and no current sophomore-eligible cycle/deadline was found. **Ruled out — no currently-open, sophomore-eligible award found.**
- **Citadel / Jane Street**: "The Citadel" search results were a military college (unrelated). Jane Street's real HBCU commitment is a $10 million gift to the Student Freedom Initiative funding income-contingent agreements (ICAs) — a financing/repayment structure, not a scholarship or grant — plus a non-cash "FOCUS" mentoring program for up to 150 HBCU students/year in tech and finance. **Ruled out — not a cash scholarship; ICAs require income-based repayment after graduation, a materially different (and riskier) financial product than every other tracked entry.**
- **UBS / Deutsche Bank (general)**: No current UBS or Deutsche Bank scholarship was found targeting Black undergraduates in finance who are sophomores. Deutsche Bank's "Accelerate Scholarship" for Black heritage students is a defunct (2012-era), UK-only program. UBS's own "UBS Scholars Program" is restricted to four specific North Carolina schools with no race-specific criteria. **Ruled out — no qualifying current program found.**
- **Lazard Undergraduate Diversity Fellowship**: A real fellowship for second-year (sophomore) undergraduates ($5,000 paid in third year + $5,000 on accepting a full-time offer), but — like the already-tracked Morgan Stanley Fisher and JPMorgan scholarships — it is gated behind first completing Lazard's interview process and receiving a Summer Analyst Investment Banking offer. No explicit race/diversity-population criteria were confirmed in this round's sources (only "underrepresented" implied by program branding), and no current 2026-2027 deadline could be verified — the only dated listing found had closed in March 2023. **Not added — insufficiently distinct from and insufficiently verified relative to the already-tracked bank-internship-gated scholarships; revisit if a clearer, currently-dated source surfaces.**

## One lead investigated but not added for insufficient verification

- **New Jersey Bankers Education Foundation Scholarship**: A real NJ-residency scholarship (via the New Jersey Bankers Association) appears in multiple third-party aggregators (CollegeXpress, CollegeScholarships.org), open to NJ residents pursuing a college degree with no confirmed major restriction — potentially a good `nj-resident` fit. However, no official njbankers.com page could be found in this round's searches to confirm the award amount, and the aggregator listings contain internally inconsistent/possibly-mixed-up eligibility language (one cites unrelated military-residence criteria that may belong to a different NJ scholarship entirely). Per the "never fabricate a scholarship" rule, not added this round pending a direct check of njbankers.com or a phone/email confirmation. **Follow up next round** by searching specifically for the foundation's own scholarship application page rather than aggregator mirrors.
