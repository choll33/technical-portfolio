## One new scholarship added

- **TMCF | Enterprise Access HBCU Scholarship** (`tmcf-enterprise-access-hbcu`, `howard-hbcu`): A need-based TMCF/Enterprise award, open to full-time undergraduates at any TMCF member HBCU (Howard qualifies) in **any major** — up to $15,000, 81 scholars per cycle. Unlike most individual TMCF sponsor sub-awards found in prior sessions (Sprott, Verisk, CoBank, FedEx, NRG — all with narrow windows that had already closed by the time they were found), this one has a long, currently-open application window: the "2027-2028" cycle runs Aug 3, 2026 – Jan 8, 2027, confirmed via search summary of TMCF's own page. The prior "2026-2027" cycle (Sept 15, 2025 – Jan 16, 2026) is closed. Added as its own entry (distinct from the general `tmcf-open-portal` entry) because it's a clean, all-majors, currently-open match with a confirmed deadline — most other TMCF sponsor awards reviewed to date are either major-restricted, school-restricted, or already closed.
