## New leads investigated and ruled out (2 this round)

- **New Jersey Educational Opportunity Fund (NJEOF)**: A real, long-running NJ state aid program surfaced in this round's general NJ-scholarship sweep. Directly confirmed via NJ state and campus EOF pages that EOF funding **cannot follow a student to an out-of-state school** — it only supports students enrolled at participating New Jersey institutions, with admission handled at the campus level by each school's EOF office. Since Mason attends Howard University (Washington, D.C., not NJ), he is not eligible regardless of his NJ residency or financial-need profile. **Ruled out — do not re-add unless Mason transfers to a NJ institution.**
