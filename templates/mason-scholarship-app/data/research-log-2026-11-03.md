# Scholarship Research for Mason — Update, November 3, 2026

Follow-up to the November 2, 2026 research log. Mason remains a rising sophomore Finance major at Howard University (School of Business), GPA 3.7, NJ resident/hometown. Tracker held 72 entries entering this round.

This round focused on (a) a fresh sweep across all five priority tiers (Howard/HBCU-specific, Black finance/business, NJ-resident, national finance-major, general) for new scholarships, and (b) re-verifying a batch of standing null-deadline entries not covered in yesterday's re-check: `rothschild-black-leaders`, `pimco-future-leaders`, `gm-financial-sophomore-leadership`, `citi-early-id-program`, `jpmorgan-launching-leaders`.

## One new entry added

- **`jpmorgan-chase-wealth-management-hbcu-scholarship`** (category: `howard-hbcu`): the J.P. Morgan Wealth Management HBCU Scholarship Program, run with UNCF since 2021. This is a genuinely new find, distinct from the already-tracked JPMorgan Launching Leaders Scholarship (IB/Sales & Trading track) and JPMorganChase Fellowship (5-week sophomore fellowship). It's specifically for African American rising sophomores interested in financial planning/wealth management, at a defined list of partner HBCUs — and critically, a 2022 UNCF press release explicitly names **Howard University** among the 17 eligible schools that year, which is a confirmed, direct, documented match (not an assumption). Awards: up to $7,000 plus a summer educational experience toward becoming a financial advisor. Caveat: the program launched as a 5-year (2021-2025) commitment, and the most recent dated cycle found (via a College Board BigFuture listing, opened Feb 14, 2024) doesn't confirm whether a cohort continued into 2026-2027 — deadline left `null` pending confirmation that the program is still running. Worth a direct check of opportunities.uncf.org or a call to Howard's financial aid office to ask if they've seen this program recently.

## New leads investigated and ruled out this round

- **American College of Financial Services HBCU Scholarship**: investigated as a promising-sounding lead (confirmed real via financial-planning.com and jbhe.com coverage: "25 scholarships, including 15 new ones for HBCU students," eligibility = Black undergraduates who've completed their sophomore year). **Ruled out** on closer inspection: this scholarship funds tuition/training at The American College of Financial Services itself — toward its RICP, ChFC, and CFP-related designations — not scholarship money toward a student's bachelor's degree at their own school (Howard). It's structurally a professional-certification scholarship, not a Howard-tuition scholarship, so it doesn't fit Mason's situation the way the LeCount Davis/Aspiriant/Goldman Scholars CFP entries might (those are contingent on Howard having a CFP Registered Program, which is a different and still-unresolved question). Not added.
- **Several New Jersey university-specific business/finance scholarships** (Provident Charities Annual Scholarship via William Paterson, Kean University College of Business scholarships, Rutgers Business School awards (Hamelsky, Tuckman, 75th Anniversary)): all confirmed to be restricted to students *enrolled at that specific NJ university* (William Paterson, Kean, or Rutgers respectively), not open to NJ residents attending college elsewhere. **Ruled out** — Mason attends Howard, not an NJ public university, so none of these apply regardless of his NJ residency.
- **North Carolina CPA Foundation Scholarship and NYSSCPA Excellence in Accounting Scholarship**: both confirmed to be restricted to students attending college in (or residents of) North Carolina / New York respectively, with no NJ or Howard tie-in. **Ruled out** — no eligibility path for Mason.
- **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program**: confirmed real and currently recruiting for various 2027 intakes, but every current U.S. partner-university slot found is non-HBCU (e.g., UC Berkeley), and the bulk of the program is for African universities. **Ruled out** — no Howard or HBCU pathway found.
- **American Bankers Association (ABA) Foundation HBCU scholarship**: searched specifically after seeing a banking-journal headline about the ABA Foundation "encouraging HBCU students to explore banking careers," but found no actual current ABA Foundation scholarship program for HBCU students — only outreach/awareness coverage and unrelated state-level bankers'-association scholarships (Missouri, Alabama). **Not added** — insufficient evidence a real program exists.
- **Wells Fargo Sophomore Discovery Fellowship Program**: investigated as a possible new entry (paid, finance-focused, for current sophomores graduating Dec 2027/June 2028). Found that as of an October 2026 Extern update, this program has been **discontinued and replaced** by a new "Wells Fargo Sophomore Experience," for which Wells Fargo has published only an interest form and no confirmed eligibility/pay/deadline details yet. **Not added** — too little confirmed detail on the replacement program to describe reliably; worth a fresh look once Wells Fargo publishes full Sophomore Experience details.
