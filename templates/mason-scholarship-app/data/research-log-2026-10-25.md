Follow-up to the October 24, 2026 research log. Mason remains a rising sophomore Finance major at Howard University (School of Business), GPA 3.7, NJ resident/hometown. Tracker held 59 entries entering this round.

## One correction made: `inroads` deadline was wrong

The existing entry stated INROADS gives priority consideration to applications submitted by **Nov 1** (fall cycle) or **Mar 1** (spring cycle). Re-searching turned up direct quotes from INROADS' own internship FAQ (students.inroads.org/resources/internship-program-faqs/ and inroads.org/internships-program/faqs/, both via WebSearch's AI summary since inroads.org itself is still DNS-unreachable from this session) that read: *"INROADS accepts applications year round. However, for priority consideration, students should complete the application and all required steps by March 31st to compete for the upcoming summer."* No Nov 1/Mar 1 fall-spring split appears anywhere in current official materials — that prior claim looks like it was either stale or conflated with another program. Updated `deadline` to `2027-03-31` (next occurrence of the March 31 priority date, for Summer 2027 placement) and rewrote `deadlineNote` to quote the corrected policy and flag the correction.

## Two new entries added

1. **`stout-diverse-emerging-leaders`** — Stout Diverse Emerging Leaders Scholarship, $2,500, explicitly restricted to **sophomores** (not sophomore-through-senior like most other entries) from an underrepresented population, pursuing Finance, Economics, Accounting, or a business-related degree, 3.2+ GPA minimum. Confirmed real via Stout's own news releases (2023-2025 winner announcements) and its student careers page. This is one of the tightest class-year matches found so far — most finance-industry scholarships target juniors/seniors or "sophomore-and-up," but this one is sophomore-specific. Deadline timing has varied cycle to cycle (2024 window closed Sept 29, 2024); no 2026 cycle-open announcement found yet, so left `deadline: null` rather than guess. Categorized `finance-major` (diversity-preferred but not race-restricted in the sources found).

2. **`gm-financial-sophomore-leadership`** — GM Financial Sophomore Leadership Internship Program, a paid 8-week internship (not a cash scholarship) explicitly for rising sophomores/juniors, run through GM Financial's participation in the Congressional Bipartisan HBCU Caucus's HBCU Partnership Challenge. Real and documented (2021 pilot launch with Prairie View A&M/UT Arlington, a third cohort by 2023 including a Texas Southern University student) via GM Financial's own newsroom pages. Added as a funding-complement entry in the same spirit as the already-tracked INROADS/SEO/MLT internship programs. Caveat flagged in the note: no 2026/2027 cycle dates found, and Howard isn't confirmed as a current partner school — worth a direct inquiry to GM Financial's university recruiting team. Categorized `howard-hbcu` given the program's explicit HBCU-partnership framing.

## New leads investigated and ruled out (11 this round)

- **Visa Black Scholars and Jobs Program (via TMCF)**: A real, well-funded ($20,000/year) program for Black students in business/tech, but every source found describes it as an **entry program for incoming/new students** (the cohort referenced starts Fall 2022, graduating Dec 2025-May 2026) rather than something a continuing sophomore can apply to mid-degree, and no newer cohort announcement (post-2023) was found. **Ruled out — appears to be an incoming-student-only program with no confirmed open cycle for continuing students; worth rechecking if TMCF announces a new application class.**
- **BlackRock Hallac Scholarship**: Real, but restricted to Georgia Tech students only. **Ruled out — school-restricted, not transferable to Howard.**
- **BlackRock Founders Scholarship**: Real ($20,000 + summer internship), but restricted to **U.S. college seniors** only. **Ruled out — wrong class year; Mason is 2+ years from senior standing, too far out to bookmark usefully.**
- **Mastercard**: No scholarship program for Black/business students found in search results. **Ruled out — no program found.**
- **American Express**: No scholarship program for Black/business students found. **Ruled out — no program found.**
- **Provident Bank Foundation (NJ)**: Only a defunct 2004-2005 "Provident Bank Scholars" program (business majors, 3.0 GPA) was found; nothing current. **Ruled out — no current program found.**
- **Investors Bank Foundation (NJ)**: Foundation funds the NJ Association of Counties (NJAC) Foundation, which gives $500 scholarships to **county vocational-technical school graduates** continuing to a county/state college — not applicable to a 4-year HBCU business student. **Ruled out — wrong student population (vo-tech grads, not 4-year university students).**
- **Valley National Bank (NJ)**: No scholarship program for college students found at all. **Ruled out — no program found.**
- **Point72 Academy**: No scholarship found for sophomores. The only sophomore-relevant offering is an unpaid "Virtual Spring Sessions" exposure program (3 weeks, no stipend, no scholarship); the paid Academy Investment Analyst Program targets students near graduation (Dec 2026-July 2027 grads), not sophomores. **Ruled out — no scholarship exists; the one sophomore-eligible offering isn't paid and isn't a scholarship.**
- **State Farm**: The "Good Neighbor Scholarship" is high-school-senior-only; "Good Neighbor Citizenship Company Grants" are invitation-only grants to organizations, not individual students; and State Farm's own grant guidance explicitly discourages race-based targeting in its charitable giving. University-endowed State Farm scholarships exist (e.g., at Troy University) but are tied to Risk Management/Insurance majors at specific schools, not Howard Finance. **Ruled out — no individual Black/HBCU-targeted scholarship exists; closest programs are HS-senior-only or institution-specific elsewhere.**
- **Northwestern Mutual "Hometown HBCU Connection"**: Real HBCU-focused scholarship, but restricted to graduating seniors of specific **Milwaukee-area high schools** (or participants in two named Milwaukee youth programs) — a geographic entry requirement Mason, already an enrolled Howard sophomore from NJ, cannot meet. **Ruled out — Milwaukee-area high-school-entry requirement; wrong population and wrong life stage.**
- **American College of Financial Services HBCU Scholarship**: Resurfaced in this round's searches (eligible after completing sophomore year, enrolled at a 4-year HBCU). This is already on the long-standing previously-excluded list from an earlier session — re-confirmed the "completed sophomore year" phrasing likely means rising-junior eligibility, consistent with the prior rule-out. **No change — remains ruled out, now double-confirmed.**
- **J.P. Morgan Chase Wealth Management HBCU Scholarship**: Resurfaced again; already on the long-standing previously-excluded list ("J.P. Morgan/UNCF Wealth Management Scholarship") with a closed 2024 cycle. **No change — remains ruled out.**
