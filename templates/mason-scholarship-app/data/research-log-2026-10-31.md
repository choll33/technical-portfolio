Sophomore-specific eligibility for the scholarship (versus the underlying internship postings, some of which target a 2027-2028 graduating cohort — one ahead of Mason) was not confirmed this round; deadline left `null` with a note to verify directly with PGIM's DEI team or jobs.pgim.com before investing significant time.
