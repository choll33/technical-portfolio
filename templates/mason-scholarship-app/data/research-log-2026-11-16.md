## Change 2 — added: English-Speaking Union (ESU) Luard Morse Scholarship

New entry `esu-luard-morse-scholarship` (category `howard-hbcu`). This is a genuinely new find, verified real via multiple independent sources:

- **Howard's own news site** (thedig.howard.edu) ran a story naming a Howard University sophomore as the **2026-2027 ESU Luard Morse Scholarship recipient** — direct, current confirmation that Howard participates and that the program targets sophomores specifically.
- **Structure**: open to sophomores at HBCUs (Howard, Hampton, and other HBCU/UNCF institutions named across sources), any major, minimum 3.0 GPA, funds a semester of study at a British university during the recipient's *junior* year. Award reported as $25,000 in older materials, up to $30,000 in more recent ESU pages (per a 2026 announcement about a different Luard Morse winner).
- **Key mechanic**: applications are submitted by a campus coordinator, not directly by the student — Howard's winner applied through the ACE Scholars Program, which is the concrete next step for Mason (ask ACE Scholars whether Howard is participating in the next cycle and what Howard's internal deadline is).
- **Deadline**: left `null`. One search result quoted what it described as ESU's own page stating the deadline is "Thursday, December 10, 2026" — this is internally consistent (Dec 10, 2026 is in fact a Thursday), which is a modest positive signal, but other aggregators (HBCUConnect, Scholarships.com) cite conflicting Dec 4 or Dec 12 dates with no year specified. Given Stanley Scott is already confirmed as the "2026-2027" recipient, it's genuinely unclear whether a Dec 2026 deadline belongs to an already-closed cycle or the next open one. esuus.org and its legacy/branch subdomains (esu.fcny.org, legacy.esuus.org) were all blocked from direct fetch this session, so this couldn't be resolved further via WebFetch — only WebSearch summaries were available.
