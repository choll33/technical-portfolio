## Change — added: Project ACCLAIM (UNCF, at Howard University School of Business)

New entry `uncf-project-acclaim-howard` (category `howard-hbcu`). Surfaced while sweeping Howard's School of Business news for anything new this round — not previously tracked. A $10 million (now $20 million after a 2026 expansion gift) UNCF initiative funded by Adage Capital Management and Elizabeth and Phill Gross, embedding hands-on asset-management training into participating HBCU finance curricula. Howard was one of the two original fall 2025 launch sites (alongside Morehouse), giving students direct exposure to managing a real student investment pool.

- Confirmed real via UNCF's own site, Howard's thedig.howard.edu, and corroborating press (Diverse: Issues in Higher Education, Black Enterprise, AfroTech, HBCU Buzz).

No student-facing sign-up page, GPA requirement, or deadline was found in search results — looks like a curriculum-embedded cohort program, similar in kind to the already-tracked AltFinance Fellowship and HPS Partners Endowed Scholars Program.
