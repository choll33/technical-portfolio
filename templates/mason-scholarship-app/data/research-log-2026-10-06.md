## New scholarships/programs added (2)

1. **J.P. Morgan Launching Leaders Undergraduate Scholarship** (`jpmorgan-launching-leaders`, `black-finance`). Up to $15,000 scholarship paired with a guaranteed Summer Analyst internship placement (Investment Banking, Sales & Trading, CIB Risk, and other CIB tracks) for Black, Hispanic, and Native American sophomores/juniors, 3.5+ GPA, any major. This is a **distinct program from the already-tracked `jpmc-fellowship`** (the race-neutral, 5-week ABP Fellowship with a lower 3.2 GPA bar and no guaranteed internship) — Launching Leaders pays more, is race-specific (so a stronger match given Mason's background), and explicitly includes Investment Banking placement. Deadline not pinned down (JPMorgan's fall recruiting cycle, same general window as the ABP Fellowship) — set to `null` with a note to watch careers.jpmorganchase.com from ~November 2026.
2. **Rothschild & Co — Global Advisory Black Leaders Program** (`rothschild-black-leaders`, `black-finance`). A 4-month NY-based career-development program for Black/African American students interested in investment banking, with technical training, mentorship, and a financial award component. Search results explicitly confirmed sophomores are eligible ("designed to educate, train, and inspire talented, ambitious sophomores"). Deadline not found this round (prior cycle was branded "US 2024/25") — set to `null` with a note to check rothschildandco.com/en/careers directly.
