## New scholarship added

1. **Aspiriant Impact Scholarship** (`aspiriant-impact-scholarship`, category `black-finance`). A CFP Board Center for Financial Planning scholarship (like the already-tracked LeCount R. Davis award) for students from populations underrepresented in financial planning — defined by gender, race/ethnicity, disability, or sexual orientation, so Mason qualifies under the race/ethnicity criterion without the LeCount Davis scholarship's Black/African-American-only restriction. Up to $10,000 for undergraduate CFP Board Registered Program candidates. The 2026 cycle's deadline (Sept 30, 2026) had just closed as of this review (2 days prior); set `deadline` to Sept 30, 2027 as an estimate for the next annual cycle. Same open caveat as LeCount Davis: contingent on Mason enrolling in (or Howard offering) a CFP Board Registered Program — worth confirming with Howard's finance department either way, since it would unlock two scholarships at once.

## Re-investigated previously-excluded program — still ruled out, with a clearer reason this time

- **Edward M. Nagel Endowed Scholarship (UNCF)**: a fresh search surfaced what looked like a second, non-freshman variant of this award (undergraduate, business major, 3.0+ GPA, U.S. residency instead of the freshman-only version's California-residency requirement), which raised the question of whether it might now fit Mason since the CA-residency and freshman restrictions seemed to not apply to this version. A follow-up search confirmed, however, that **both the freshman and the general/continuing-student version require attendance at a UNCF-member institution** — and Howard is not a UNCF member school (same reason `UNCF General Scholarship` and `pg-general-scholarship`'s HBCU-non-restriction already noted). So the exclusion stands, just for the UNCF-member-school reason alone rather than all three original reasons (freshman-only + CA-resident + UNCF-member). Not added.

## Investigated and deferred (not yet eligible — not added)
