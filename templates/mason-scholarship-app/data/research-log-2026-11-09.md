## New leads investigated and not added

- **AICPA Scholarship for Minority Accounting Students** — A real, substantial scholarship (sources disagree on amount: $3,000–$10,000, some citing 60 awards/year) with a plausible March 15, 2027 deadline (cycle typically opens Dec 1). However, eligibility is genuinely ambiguous for Mason: most sources describe it as for accounting majors who intend to pursue CPA licensure; only one secondary source suggested "accounting, finance, or related majors." Mason is a Finance major with no stated CPA intent. Given the conflicting eligibility language and the never-fabricate-eligibility standard, **not added this round** — worth a direct check of AICPA's own ThisWayToCPA platform/page before the cycle opens, since if finance majors not pursuing the CPA truly qualify this could be a strong match (3.7 GPA comfortably clears any likely minimum).
- **Lakeland Bank Annual Scholarship / Lakeland Bank Scholarship** — A concrete-sounding NJ bank scholarship for finance/accounting majors (3.0+ GPA, NJ residency, financial need) that initially looked promising for the `nj-resident` tier. On closer investigation, every instance of this scholarship found (William Paterson University, SUNY Orange, Bergen Community College, Ramapo College) is administered through that specific partner college's own AcademicWorks portal, gated to students attending one of those schools or residing in Lakeland Bank's northern-NJ service-area counties (Bergen, Essex, Morris, Ocean, Passaic, Somerset, Sussex, Union, Warren) — it is **not** a general direct-apply scholarship open to any NJ resident at any college. Howard University is not one of Lakeland's partner institutions, and Mason's NJ residency alone does not appear sufficient without attending one of those schools. **Not added** — this looks structurally similar to the already-ruled-out NJ Utilities Association pattern (employer/institution-gated, not transferable to Howard students). Not adding to the confirmed-ruled-out list below since it's NJ-bank-specific rather than one of the standing nationally-known names, but noting here so a future run doesn't re-investigate it from scratch.
- **RIMS New Jersey Chapter Scholarship** and **CPCU Society New Jersey Chapter Scholarship** — Both are real NJ-linked awards (up to $10,000 and $1,000 respectively) but target insurance/risk-management majors and curricula specifically, not finance generally. Mason's Finance major is adjacent but not a clean match, and no source confirmed broader eligibility. **Not added** — low enough confidence on fit that this isn't worth re-chasing unless Mason develops a specific insurance/risk-management interest.
- **Greenwood Project — Investment/Fintech Academy Scholar Program** — Reconfirmed, same finding as Nov 8: current postings (Investment Academy and Fintech Academy) both explicitly target the **"2028" graduating class**, one cohort ahead of Mason (2029 grad). Still not a fit this cycle; revisit when Greenwood posts a "2029" cohort.
- **Franklin Park Scholarship Program** — Reconfirmed, same contradictory status as Nov 8: the Scholarships.com/Niche listing is marked "permanently closed" yet still displays a Feb 28, 2027 calendar deadline for a $2,500 minority-business/finance award.
