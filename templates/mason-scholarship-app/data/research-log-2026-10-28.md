# Scholarship Research for Mason — Update, October 28, 2026

Follow-up to the October 27, 2026 research log. Mason remains a rising sophomore Finance major at Howard University (School of Business), GPA 3.7, NJ resident/hometown. Tracker held 61 entries entering this round.

This round focused on a fresh sweep of major financial-services firms and professional-services/accounting firms not yet checked by name in prior logs: Bank of America, Deloitte (incl. the Deloitte Foundation/TMCF track), Fidelity Investments, BlackRock, Wells Fargo (outside the already-tracked TMCF sponsor awards), the SIFMA Foundation, Visa, Mastercard, PwC/EY/KPMG, Northwestern Mutual, State Street, and Vanguard — plus a check for New Jersey bank-foundation scholarships (Cornerstone Bank, OceanFirst Foundation, CME Group Foundation Scholars) not previously investigated by name.

## Two new entries added

- **`tmcf-deloitte-foundation-accounting`** (category: `howard-hbcu`): TMCF | Deloitte Foundation Accounting Scholarship Program, Cohort 5 (renewable 2026-2029). $8,000/year, 71 scholars, explicitly open to **freshmen and sophomores** in 4-year programs (most other individual TMCF sponsor awards reviewed in prior logs required junior+ standing or were all-class-year — this one specifically calls out the sophomore window). Business/finance/accounting/economics majors eligible, 3.0+ GPA (3.3+ preferred), HBCU required (Howard qualifies). The 2026-2027 cycle (Mar 9 – Apr 24, 2026) has already closed; deadline set to an estimated Apr 24, 2027 based on the recurring annual cohort pattern (Cohort 1 through 5 so far), flagged clearly as an estimate in the note.
- **`northwestern-mutual-annual-business-scholarship`** (category: `finance-major`): A national, non-race-restricted scholarship (~$2,500, varies) for business/finance/accounting/marketing/economics majors who are **juniors or seniors**. Not yet eligible as a rising sophomore — added as a bookmark for Mason's 2027-2028 junior year, following the same pattern as the already-tracked AlixPartners/UNCF, Crum & Forster/UNCF, UNCF Element Fleet, UNCF MetLife Legacy, and NBMBAA bookmark entries. Deadline left null — only aggregator-sourced (Peterson's, Sallie/Scholly), not an official Northwestern Mutual page, and the Sept 5, 2026 figure found this round has already passed with no official 2027 date published.

No existing entries' deadlines changed this round — no new confirmed current-cycle dates were found for any of the tracked null/estimated-deadline entries re-checked.

## New leads investigated and ruled out (10 this round)

- **Bank of America**: No national scholarship was found that is limited to Black undergraduates in finance who are sophomores. Every program found is school-specific (UNLV minority-business fund, UGA Terry College finance scholarship, FSU/Maine/Palm Beach County awards), with no connection to Howard. **Ruled out — no applicable national program found; school-specific awards don't cover Howard.**
- **Fidelity Scholars Program (via UNCF, "Invest in My Education")**: Real, well-funded ($250M pledge) program, but confirmed to be an **incoming-freshman-only** award (applicants must be high school seniors as of a fixed date the year before matriculating) targeting the "mighty middle" GPA band of 2.5–3.59. Mason is already a continuing sophomore and his 3.7 GPA sits above the targeted band. **Ruled out — wrong class year (freshman-entry only) and GPA band mismatch.**
- **Visa Black Scholars and Jobs Program (via TMCF)**: A strong-looking award on paper ($20,000/year, finance named as an eligible major, Black/African American students, needs-based) but every source — TMCF's own program description, Black Enterprise coverage of two prior cohorts, aggregator listings — confirms it is restricted to **current high school seniors / incoming first-year students** with a fixed Fall-matriculation graduation window. **Ruled out — incoming-freshman-only, not open to continuing undergraduates.**
- **CME Group Foundation Scholars Program**: Real, sophomore-eligible, finance-major program with a generous award (up to $20,000, renewable), but restricted to students enrolled full-time at one of its ~11-13 partner schools in Illinois and New Jersey (DePaul, Loyola Chicago, Illinois Institute of Technology, Chicago State, City Colleges of Chicago, National Louis, Northern Illinois, and similar). Howard University is not among the partner schools, and NJ residency/hometown alone doesn't satisfy the requirement — the student must *attend* a partner school. **Ruled out — partner-school-restricted; Howard is not a participating institution.**
- **SIFMA Foundation**: The SIFMA Foundation itself runs only educational-outreach programs (Stock Market Game, InvestWrite, Invest It Forward, Capitol Hill Challenge), not scholarships. A separate "SIFMA Scholarship Program" exists but is restricted to children of employees at SIFMA member firms — inapplicable to Mason regardless. **Ruled out — no scholarship program open to the general public; the one SIFMA-branded scholarship found is employee-dependents-only.**
- **Wells Fargo (non-TMCF)**: Beyond the already-tracked TMCF | Wells Fargo Access and Gap Scholarships, no additional national Wells Fargo scholarship targeting Black students in business/finance and open to sophomores was found. The closest match, Minnesota State's "Wells Fargo College of Business Cultural Diversity Scholarship," is a single-school award with no Black-specific restriction and no clear sophomore eligibility confirmed. **Ruled out — no additional qualifying national program found.**
- **BlackRock Founders Scholarship**: A real award ($17,500–$20,000 depending on source/year) for students from underrepresented groups (Black, Hispanic, Native American, LGBTQ+, disabled), but sources conflict irreconcilably on class-year eligibility — some describe it as open only to graduating seniors, others (older, 2019-2020-era materials) describe "rising juniors," and a University of Nebraska–Lincoln post references a separate "educational program for college sophomores" whose relationship to the Founders Scholarship itself couldn't be confirmed. Deadline/award-amount figures also varied across sources (July 31 vs. other dates; $12,500 vs. $17,500 vs. $20,000). It is also gated behind applying to and being accepted into BlackRock's Summer Analyst/internship recruiting process, not a standalone scholarship application. **Not added — could not verify current (2026) sophomore eligibility, deadline, or award amount well enough to responsibly add; worth re-checking directly at careers.blackrock.com (not blocked, but no current-cycle scholarship page was surfaced via search) next round.**
- **PwC / EY / KPMG**: No scholarship from any of the three firms was found that targets HBCU/Black sophomores specifically. PwC partners with INROADS on an "HBCU career advantage" track (3.3+ GPA preferred) but no deadline, award amount, or standalone application was surfaced — this is better treated as already covered by the existing `inroads` tracker entry rather than a separate line item. EY and KPMG's named undergraduate accounting awards found (e.g., at Pace University) are junior/senior-and-up only. KPMG's HBCU investment found is graduate-level only. **Ruled out — no sophomore-eligible undergraduate award found from any of the three; PwC's HBCU track overlaps with the already-tracked INROADS entry.**
- **Northwestern Mutual Hometown HBCU Connection**: A real, actively-funded HBCU scholarship (~$500K multi-year commitment), but restricted to high school seniors graduating from specific Milwaukee-area high schools. **Ruled out — wrong population (HS seniors) and wrong geography (Milwaukee-area only), not transferable to an NJ-resident Howard sophomore.**
- **Mastercard, State Street, Vanguard**: No scholarship program open to undergraduates was found under any of these three names in this round's searches. **Ruled out — no program found.**
