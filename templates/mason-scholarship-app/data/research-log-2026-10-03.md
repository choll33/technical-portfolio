## New scholarships added (4, all `general` category)

1. **1 for 2 Education Foundation Scholarship** (`one-for-2-education-foundation`). Up to $25,000, covering remaining tuition after other aid. Minimum GPA cited as 3.5 by one source and 3.7 by another — Mason's 3.7 clears both, so included without caveat on GPA fit. Open to full-time undergraduates at any U.S. four-year school, no major restriction, no citizenship requirement. Deadline set to Feb 1, 2027 based on a consistent recurring Dec-open/Feb-close pattern across multiple aggregators. Notable "pay it forward" condition: recipients pledge to fund a comparable scholarship for two unrelated people someday.
2. **Bold.org "Be Bold" No-Essay Scholarship** (`bold-be-bold-no-essay`). $25,000, no essay, no GPA/major restriction, rolling monthly deadlines (current: Oct 31, 2026). Verified Bold.org is a legitimate 501(c)(3)-backed platform (checked via Scholarships360, DeleteMe, and legitimacy-review sites) that pays schools directly with no fees — not a sweepstakes scam, just extremely high-volume/low-odds given the no-essay format.
3. **Niche $40,000 "No Essay" Scholarship** (`niche-40k-no-essay`). $40,000 random-drawing award from Niche (an established college-search company also behind Niche.com rankings), open to any student headed to or in college. Current cycle deadline Oct 15, 2026 — recurs annually, so flagged in the note to check again in fall 2027 if missed.
4. **SoFi $2,500 Scholarship Giveaway** (`sofi-scholarship-giveaway`). $2,500/month no-essay sweepstakes from SoFi, open to any enrolled/admitted student at an accredited 4-year school. No fixed deadline — monthly rolling entry periods through the 2026 calendar year. Complements the already-tracked Sallie monthly scholarship with a second, independent monthly entry.

These round out the "general, no major restriction, 3.7 GPA qualifies easily" tier with low-effort, zero-cost options Mason can stack alongside the more competitive HBCU/Black-finance applications.
