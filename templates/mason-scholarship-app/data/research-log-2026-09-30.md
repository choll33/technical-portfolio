Follow-up to the September 29, 2026 research log (run the previous day). Mason remains a rising sophomore Finance major at Howard University (School of Business), GPA 3.7, NJ resident/hometown. Given how thorough the last two consecutive daily passes were, this round focused on (a) probing a few previously-unexplored corners — Ivy/bank-run diversity scholarship-plus-internship programs, NJ-specific leads, and a couple of Howard-specific search angles — and (b) light re-verification of the weakest-confidence deadlines (NABA, Howard endowed scholarship).

## New scholarships added

1. **Goldman Sachs Scholarship for Excellence (SFE)** (`goldman-sachs-sfe`, category `black-finance`). A diversity-recruiting scholarship for Black, Hispanic, and Native American undergraduates explicitly open to sophomores/juniors ("penultimate year"), min 3.4 GPA, pairing a paid GS summer internship with up to $15,000 toward tuition. Strong fit for Mason's class year, GPA, and finance interest. Confirmed recurring cycle: opens Nov 1, closes Jan 1, winners announced Mar 1 — set deadline to Jan 1, 2027 for the upcoming window. Apply via goldmansachs.com/careers (events portal, "Interviews" event type).
2. **Morgan Stanley Richard B. Fisher Scholarship Program** (`morgan-stanley-fisher`, category `black-finance`). $15,000 award for Black, Hispanic, Native American, and LGBT sophomores/juniors (3.0+ GPA, any major), bundled with a Morgan Stanley summer internship. The scholarship application only unlocks after applying to an open Summer Analyst role, and sources disagreed on a single fixed deadline (citing late June, early October, and early November across different tracks/years) — left `deadline: null` with a note to watch for Summer 2027 Analyst postings opening in fall 2026.
3. **JPMorganChase Fellowship Program** (`jpmc-fellowship`, category `finance-major`). Not a cash scholarship — a paid, 5-week summer fellowship for sophomores across finance/business/tech tracks (Commercial & Investment Bank, Global Markets, Corporate Function, etc.) that leads to summer-analyst-internship consideration the next year. Distinct from the bank scholarships above in that JPMC states it's open to students of all backgrounds, not diversity-restricted — included under `finance-major` rather than `black-finance` for that reason. The 2026 cohort's deadline was Jan 12–13, 2026 (already closed); estimated Jan 12, 2027 for the next cycle based on that pattern.

## Investigated and explicitly ruled out this round (do not re-add without new evidence)

- **CME Group Foundation Scholars Program** (renewable, up to $20,000/year, Black/Latinx finance & tech majors, low-income + first-generation): looked very promising at first (explicitly names NJ), but confirmed via search that it is restricted to a fixed list of ~13 partner schools, almost entirely Illinois institutions plus Stevens Institute of Technology (NJ) — **Howard University is not one of the partner schools**, so Mason cannot apply regardless of major/income/first-gen status. Not added.
- **Edward M. Nagel Endowed Scholarship (UNCF-administered)**: initially looked like another UNCF-administered program open broadly (like the already-tracked `pg-hbcyou`/`pg-general-scholarship`), but confirmed this one is restricted to **entering freshmen** at **UNCF member schools** who are **California residents** — three separate disqualifiers for Mason (rising sophomore, Howard isn't a UNCF member school, NJ not CA resident). Not added.
- **Alliance of Black Women Accountants (ABWA) Scholarship**: confirmed restricted to Black women pursuing accounting/finance careers. Not added (gender-restricted; also accounting-leaning, similar to the already-excluded NJCPA fund).
- **NJCPA Scholarship Fund**: resurfaced in a general NJ-scholarship search; re-confirmed it is restricted to accounting majors (24+ accounting credits), same disqualifying reason as the prior "NJCPA Scholarship Foundation" ruling — same organization, not a fit for a Finance major. No change to prior exclusion.
- **Toigo Foundation**: resurfaced in a search for undergraduate finance diversity programs; re-confirmed it is an MBA/graduate-level fellowship only (2-year full-time MBA, JD/MBA, or Masters in Finance/Real Estate applicants). No change to prior exclusion.
