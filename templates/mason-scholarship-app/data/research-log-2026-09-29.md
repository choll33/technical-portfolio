## New scholarships added

1. **Procter & Gamble HBCYOU Scholarship Program** (`pg-hbcyou`, category `howard-hbcu`). A P&G-funded, UNCF-*administered* (not UNCF-*restricted*) need-based scholarship up to $2,500, open to full-time undergraduates at **any** accredited four-year HBCU in the U.S. — explicitly not limited to UNCF's 37 member schools, which is the distinction that disqualified the previously-ruled-out UNCF General Scholarship and TJX/UNCF scholarship. Confirmed via multiple independent aggregator listings (Access Scholarships, Financial Aid Finder, NCCU's own AcademicWorks posting) that eligibility is "any U.S. located, accredited, four-year HBCU" and open to all class years (freshman through 5th-year), so Howard and Mason's sophomore standing both qualify. Deadline pattern (Dec 15) is consistent across at least two recent cycles (2024-25 and 2025-26), so Dec 15, 2026 is a confident estimate for the next cycle.
2. **Procter & Gamble General Scholarship** (`pg-general-scholarship`, category `finance-major`). A separate, non-HBCU-restricted $5,000 UNCF-administered award for undergraduates in business, IT, or public relations — finance is explicitly named as an eligible major, and eligible class years run freshman through senior/5th-year. Added despite unresolved deadline conflict (search results disagreed between a Feb 9–Mar 9 window and an April 25, 2024 date for the 2023-24 cycle) — flagged clearly in the deadlineNote rather than guessing.
