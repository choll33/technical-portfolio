Follow-up to the October 26, 2026 research log. Mason remains a rising sophomore Finance major at Howard University (School of Business), GPA 3.7, NJ resident/hometown. Tracker holds 60 entries entering this round.

This round focused on (a) re-checking a handful of existing entries with unconfirmed/null deadlines that were flagged as potentially time-sensitive, and (b) a fresh sweep of major banks and finance-adjacent organizations not yet checked by name: Prudential Financial, Capital One, Ally Financial, TD Bank, PNC Bank, American Bankers Association Foundation, National Bankers Association, National Urban League, NAACP, and Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac.

Every lead investigated this round either turned out not to be a fit for Mason, or could not be verified well enough to responsibly add (see below). No existing entry's deadline changed.

## New leads investigated and ruled out (11 this round)

- **Prudential Financial (general undergraduate scholarship, distinct from the already-excluded Prudential Emerging Visionaries)**: No separate current Prudential undergraduate scholarship was found. The only other Prudential-funded program surfaced was the Prudential Scholars Program at Rutgers University–Newark, a 2019-established full-ride honors cohort restricted to Newark, NJ residents attending Rutgers–Newark specifically. **Ruled out — school-restricted to Rutgers–Newark, not transferable to Howard, and not a general program Mason could apply to.**
- **National Bankers Association (trade group for Black-owned banks) scholarship**: No scholarship program was found under this name in this round's searches; all results returned were unrelated state bankers' associations (Nebraska, Texas, North Carolina). **Ruled out — no program found.**
- **NAACP Roy Wilkins Scholarship**: Real award exists ($1,000/year, non-renewable) but could not be verified well enough to add responsibly. Sources conflict on basic eligibility: one scholarship directory describes it as for "entering college freshmen" only, while a guidance-office summary implies continuing students may apply; GPA minimums cited range from 2.5 to 3.0 depending on source; naacp.org itself (and naacp.org/scholarships) could not be reached directly this session (DNS/network block), so none of this could be confirmed against an official page. **Not added — eligibility for a continuing sophomore specifically is unconfirmed, and the conflicting third-party details are too inconsistent to trust without the official source. Worth a direct check at naacp.org/scholarships or a call to the NAACP Education Department (410-580-5760) next round.**
- **Capital One diversity/sophomore scholarship**: The only diversity-specific Capital One scholarship found is a 2014 flyer for STEM-majoring sophomores — roughly a decade stale, no evidence it still runs. Current Capital One programs found (UT Dallas applied-research scholarship, HBCU Week scholarship for incoming freshmen) don't fit. **Ruled out — no current program found; closest historical match is stale and unconfirmed to still exist.**
- **Ally Financial**: Four distinct Ally-funded programs found, none a fit — (1) Ally Financial Law Scholars Program (via CBCF) is law-school/legal-career-track only; (2) Ally Financial Public Policy Scholars Program is explicitly for Black sophomores/juniors but restricted to a policy major, not finance; (3) an SIU Carbondale Ally Automotive Student Scholarship is Automotive Technology-major only; (4) "Moguls in the Making" is an HBCU business-plan pitch competition, not an individual scholarship Mason could apply to solo. **Ruled out — major mismatch on the two individual-scholarship programs, wrong major on the automotive award, and the HBCU program is a team competition rather than an individual award.**
- **American Bankers Association (ABA) Foundation scholarship**: ABA Foundation's 2026 announcements cover financial-education programming for banks/the public, not a student scholarship. No undergraduate award was found under the ABA Foundation name. **Ruled out — no program found.**
- **National Urban League scholarship**: The main NUL Scholarship is tied to Youth Council membership and/or the Project Ready program, both restricted to high school seniors, with the most recent window (closed Dec 1, 2025 per one listing, Jul 31, 2026 per another) already passed and no indication it extends to continuing college students. Local affiliate scholarships (e.g., NY Urban League's Whitney M. Young Jr. Scholarship) are themselves HS-senior/NYC-resident-restricted. **Ruled out — wrong population (HS seniors / Youth Council members), not applicable to a continuing college sophomore.**
- **Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac scholarship**: No scholarship program was found under either name. Freddie Mac's HBCU-facing work is a financial-literacy curriculum partnership (Center for Financial Advancement via HomeFree-USA), not a scholarship; the one Fannie Mae program found (American Dream Team) is an old, apparently-discontinued professional pipeline, not a current student award. **Ruled out — no scholarship program found at either organization.**
- **Discover Financial Services**: Two programs found — (1) the Discover Diversity Accelerator is an unpaid, multi-day career-exposure program for sophomores, not a cash scholarship; (2) the Discover $5,000 No-Essay Scholarship Sweepstakes is real and general (not diversity-targeted) but its 2025-2026 cycle ran Sept 2, 2025 – Aug 31, 2026 and has already closed, with no 2026-2027 cycle announcement found yet. **Ruled out for now — the Accelerator isn't a scholarship, and the Sweepstakes has no confirmed open cycle; revisit discover.com/scholarship in a future round in case a new cycle opens.**
- **TD Bank scholarships**: Every TD-branded scholarship found (Scholarship for Indigenous Peoples, Scholarships for Community Leadership) is a Canadian program for Canadian students. No U.S. TD Bank student scholarship was found. **Ruled out — Canada-only, not applicable to a U.S. student.**
- **PNC Bank**: Three distinct PNC programs found, none a fit — (1) the 2026 NC HBCU Initiative is an institutional grant to five named North Carolina HBCUs for entrepreneurship/workforce programming, not a direct-to-student scholarship, and Howard isn't a partner school; (2) the PNC Grow Up Great Scholarship is restricted to students pursuing an early-childhood-education/childcare credential; (3) the PNC Student Lending Scholarship Sweepstakes requires the entrant to be a current PNC Solution Loan borrower, which doesn't apply to Mason absent that loan relationship. **Ruled out — institutional/school-restricted, wrong field of study, or requires an unrelated existing PNC loan.**
