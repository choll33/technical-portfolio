## One new entry added

**`citi-early-id-program`** — Citi Early ID Program, a three-week virtual pre-internship experience for undergraduate sophomores (Banking or Markets track), with mentorship, training webinars, networking, and interview prep. Confirmed real and current via multiple live jobs.citi.com Fall 2026 postings: open to all majors and "all students," 3.3+ GPA preferred (Mason's 3.7 clears it), current sophomore standing required. The Fall 2026 window closed October 23, 2026 — just before this review — but Citi runs the program on a recurring Fall-and-Spring cycle (a third-party guide cites a Spring window opening around February). Left `deadline: null` since the exact Spring 2027 dates aren't posted yet. Notably, 2025-cycle postings also listed a separate HBCU Leadership affinity track alongside Banking/Markets, worth asking about when Mason applies. Categorized `finance-major` since the core program is open-to-all rather than race-restricted.

## New leads investigated and ruled out (13 this round)

- **KPMG Future Diversity Leaders (FDL) Program**: Real program historically recruiting freshmen/sophomores in business/STEM, but every source found describing an active cycle dates to ~2015; no confirmed 2026-2027 application window, deadline, or current application link was found. **Ruled out for now — no confirmed live cycle; worth rechecking closer to KPMG's typical campus recruiting season (historically ~February) in case a current posting surfaces.**
- **UBS Scholars Program (via UNCF)**: Real, open to freshmen/sophomores in business/finance/technology with up to $5,000, but restricted to four specific schools (NC A&T, NC Central, Shaw University, Winston-Salem State). **Ruled out — school-restricted, Howard not included.**
- **Fidelity Scholars Program (Invest in My Education, via UNCF)**: Real, large ($250M) initiative, but eligibility is for high school seniors/GED holders entering their *first* bachelor's or associate program, not continuing college students. **Ruled out — entry-only, not applicable to a continuing sophomore.**
- **Bank of America HBCU programs**: Only institutional grants to schools (EAB Way Forward Initiative, racial-equity commitment grants to specific HBCUs) were found, no individual student scholarship. **Ruled out — no individual-student program found.**
- **Deloitte Canada Bloom Black Student Scholarship**: Real, $5,000 + work term, but Canada-only (Canadian post-secondary enrollment required). **Ruled out — wrong country.**
- **TMCF | Deloitte Foundation Accounting Scholarship**: Real, well-funded ($8,000, 71 scholars, HBCU-wide), but explicitly limited to students intending to become or currently majoring in **accounting** specifically, not finance broadly. **Ruled out — major mismatch (accounting-only, not finance).**
- **New Jersey Bankers Education Foundation Scholarship**: Real NJBankers-sponsored award, but targets children/spouses of **servicemembers** tied to NJ residency or a NJ military base — unrelated eligibility population. **Ruled out — wrong population (military-family-tied, not a general student award).**
- **Johnson & Johnson Scholarship (via UNCF/College Board)**: Real, up to $5,000, NJ-resident-eligible, but restricted to **college juniors and seniors** and explicitly steers NJ residents toward nursing/health-science programs. **Ruled out — wrong class year (junior/senior only) and health-science-steered, not finance.**
- **Vanguard-branded scholarships**: Three distinct programs found, none a fit — (1) BlackNorth Initiative's "Vanguard Scholars" is Canadian, targets Grade 11 high schoolers, not U.S. college sophomores; (2) Prairie View A&M's "Vanguard Current-Use Scholarship Fund" is a PVAMU-only internal fund; (3) the TMCF | Vanguard Scholarship (2023-2024 cycle) targeted juniors. **Ruled out — none match Mason's country/school/class-year combination.**
- **State Street**: No State Street-sponsored HBCU or finance scholarship was found at all in this round's searches. **Ruled out — no program found.**
- **Apollo/Ares/Oaktree AltFinance Initiative**: Real, well-funded ($90M/10-year) HBCU private-equity pipeline with mentored fellowships and needs-based scholarship funding, but explicitly partnered only with Clark Atlanta University, Morehouse College, and Spelman College. **Ruled out — school-restricted, Howard not a partner school.**
- **The Carlyle Group**: No Carlyle-sponsored HBCU or finance scholarship was found; only unrelated same-name results (a Carlyle Group co-founder's personal gift to University of Chicago Law School, and an unrelated "Carlyle Tools" auto-parts scholarship). **Ruled out — no program found.**
- **Blackstone HBCU programs**: Blackstone Charitable Foundation funds "LaunchPad," an entrepreneurship/pitch-competition program (not a tuition scholarship) at six named HBCUs (Morgan State, Bowie State, NC A&T, NC Central, Clark Atlanta, Morehouse, Spelman) — Howard is not listed as a partner school, and the program isn't finance-specific or a cash scholarship in the traditional sense. **Ruled out — school-restricted (Howard not a partner) and not a finance-specific scholarship.**
- **"TMCF | Citi Exploration Program"**: Could not confirm this program exists under that name; search turned up a 2021-era TMCF/Citi Foundation career-readiness partnership of unclear current status, and separately the real, currently-active Citi Early ID Program (added above, see new entry). **Not added as a separate ruled-out line item — superseded by the Citi Early ID Program finding.**
