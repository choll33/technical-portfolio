## One new entry added: MLT Career Prep Program (Financial Services track)

Found via a general sweep for Black/Latinx finance career programs and confirmed as real and currently accepting applications: **Management Leadership for Tomorrow (MLT) — Career Prep Program, Financial Services Track** (`mlt-career-prep`, category `black-finance`). This is a free, multi-year career-accelerator (bootcamp + coaching + employer access + recruiting support), not a cash scholarship, in the same vein as the already-tracked SEO Career and INROADS entries. Key points verified across MLT's own site and multiple university career-center pages (Tufts, Harvard, Cornell, Purdue, Miami):

- Recruits **college sophomores graduating Fall 2028, Spring 2029, or Summer 2029** for the current (2026-2027) cycle — this matches Mason's class year exactly.
- Financial Services track priority deadline was **Aug 1, 2026**, already passed. MLT's prior (2025-2026) cycle ran three further rounds after its own priority deadline (Oct 1, Nov 1, Dec 1) plus a Jan 15 final deadline, so a later round for this cycle is likely still open, but no confirmed 2026-2027 date for a post-priority round was found — left `deadline` as `null` with a note explaining the pattern and urging Mason to apply immediately via mlt.org rather than wait.
- Program framing is slightly inconsistent across sources: some campus career pages describe it as for Black, Latinx, and Native American students; MLT's own page states its programs are open to all who meet the criteria regardless of demographic identity. Recorded both framings in the entry's description/requirements rather than picking one.
- No fixed GPA minimum published; Tufts notes 3.0+ is typical among competitive applicants, which Mason's 3.7 clears easily.
