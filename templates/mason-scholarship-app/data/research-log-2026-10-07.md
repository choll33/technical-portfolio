## New scholarships added (3)

1. **NABA Northern New Jersey Chapter College Scholarship** (`naba-northern-nj-chapter`, `nj-resident`). Up to $5,500 for NJ residents who are NABA student members pursuing accounting, finance, or business, 3.0+ GPA, at a college in any state. A direct geographic complement to the already-tracked NABA National Scholarship — same membership requirement serves both. Only one past-cycle data point was found (2024: opened Feb 19, closed Apr 19), not enough to project a confident 2026-2027 date, so `deadline` is `null` rather than guessed.
2. **Colgate-Palmolive "Inspire Our Future" Commercial Scholarship** (`colgate-palmolive-inspire-our-future-commercial`, `black-finance`). $10,000, Black/African American college sophomores/juniors pursuing business, communications, finance, or marketing (finance explicitly named), 2.80+ GPA. Confirmed via multiple sources that attendance at one of the program's 66 partner schools is a stated *preference*, not a requirement — the scholarship is open to any accredited four-year U.S. college, so Howard students can apply despite no partner-school affiliation. Only one confirmed cycle (Feb 1–Apr 3, 2025) was found; no 2026 cycle announcement surfaced, so the Apr 3, 2027 estimate carries more uncertainty than most tracked entries — flagged accordingly in its note.
3. **Byron and Michelle Johnson Scholarship** (`byron-michelle-johnson-scholarship`, `nj-resident`). Bold.org-hosted scholarship for HBCU students who are underrepresented minorities from New Jersey or Pennsylvania, 2.5+ GPA, essay on NJ/PA life experience. Direct match on both the HBCU and NJ-residency axes. Only one cycle found (deadline July 22, 2025); award amount not surfaced. `deadline` set to `null` given the single data point.

Flagged the discrepancy (existing Jan 28–Mar 6, 2027 estimate vs. this April 6 data point) in the note for a future session to resolve once business.howard.edu is reachable, rather than silently overwriting the existing estimate on an unclear match.
