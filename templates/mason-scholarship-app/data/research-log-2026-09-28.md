Follow-up to the July 14, 2026 research log. Mason is now a current sophomore Finance major at Howard University (School of Business), GPA 3.7, NJ resident/hometown. This pass re-verified deadlines on all 17 previously tracked scholarships and searched for new opportunities across the same five priority tiers.

**Network note:** Direct fetches of most organization sites (tmcf.org, nabainc.org, cbcfinc.org, business.howard.edu, financialservices.howard.edu, bold.org, kitces.com) were blocked by this session's network egress proxy. A Bold.org-hosted scholarship restricted to Howard University undergraduates (freshman–junior, 3.0+ GPA) pursuing a business or related degree. Matches Mason's school, major, and class year exactly. Most recent cycle's deadline (April 5, 2026) already passed; flagged uncertainty about whether it recurs annually since it's donor-funded through Bold.org rather than a standing institutional program.
